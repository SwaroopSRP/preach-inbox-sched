# Privacy Policy for PreachInbox

**Last Updated:** September 2026

PreachInbox ("we", "our", or "us") operates the PreachInbox Email Job Scheduler service. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect minimal information to provide and improve our service:
- **Google Account Information**: When you sign in with Google, we access your name, email address, and profile picture (`openid`, `email`, `profile` scopes) solely to identify you and manage your scheduled emails.
- **Email Data**: Email recipients, subjects, and bodies submitted for scheduling.

### 2. Google API User Data Policy
PreachInbox adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy:
- We only access standard profile information required for authentication.
- We do not share or sell user data to third parties or advertising networks.
- We do not use Google user data for training generalized AI/ML models without explicit consent.

### 3. Data Storage and Security
All tokens and credentials are encrypted. Passwords and session tokens are stored using secure HTTP-only cookies.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:
